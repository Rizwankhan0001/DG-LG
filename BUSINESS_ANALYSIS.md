Analysis date: 18 September 2026. Product information was checked against [Dhampur Green's website](https://www.dhampurgreen.com/) and its public catalogue. The public Shopify feed returned 218 products at the time of review; the application curates 15 items particularly relevant to hospitality.

## What your website supports

Dhampur Green sells specialty sugars, jaggery and khand, beverage ingredients, syrups and toppings, mouth fresheners and other foods. It already has a dedicated [HORECA/B2B collection](https://www.dhampurgreen.com/collections/horeca-b2b) with portioned sweeteners and food-service products. That makes ingredient replenishment and hospitality service a useful focus for prospecting.

The website is also a retail catalogue. Public retail prices and first-listed variants should not automatically become commercial quotes. The application labels these as references and leaves B2B pricing, quantities and negotiated opportunity values to your team.

## Your initial market order

The user selected **Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Bengaluru**. Start with a manageable city/category combination, such as Delhi NCR cafés, then compare the quality of conversations and sample requests before increasing search volume. The software supports additional Indian cities, but it does not claim that any city has a verified demand advantage.

Delhi NCR includes the broader Delhi, Gurugram and Noida market in discovery queries. Search results depend on provider coverage; searches are not a census of all hospitality businesses. Extend coverage through more specific neighbourhood searches or approved commercial data sources if you later need exhaustive territory coverage.

## Turn discovery into sales activity

1. Find public business listings and save their sources.
2. Prioritize category fit and accessible business contact channels.
3. Confirm who handles purchasing and whether your relevant ingredients are used.
4. Discuss samples, commercial pack sizes, expected consumption and delivery constraints.
5. Record the next action and a realistic opportunity value only once requirements are clearer.
6. Track sample feedback, negotiation and a won agreement; verify actual invoicing and collected revenue in your accounting system.

Automating the first steps should create more time for the conversations that determine whether a lead becomes revenue. The first release automates discovery, deduplication, scoring and draft preparation. It keeps sending in the reviewed outreach workflow and records follow-up dates in the CRM.

## Commercial details to supply next

- B2B price list by pack size, case size and minimum order quantity.
- Shipping coverage, lead times and any city-specific distribution partners.
- Priority products, inventory availability and desired margin ranges.
- Sample pack policy, sales contact details and the mailbox receiving replies.
- Monthly sales target and a realistic average monthly order assumption.

These details would improve quotes and planning. The current platform does not invent them or promise a revenue increase.
