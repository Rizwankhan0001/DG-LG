## Sorting and outreach preparation

- Search by company, product, ingredient, person or published role.
- Filter companies with procurement, R&D, sales, leadership or any named professional leads.
- Sort companies by name, research check date, matched product count, named contacts or purchasing contacts first. Product view supports title and source-date sorting.
- Within a company, filter people by department and source; sort by job title, person name, check date, company sources first or purchasing/R&D relevance.
- Each person has a suggested next step appropriate to the published role. Research check dates are not appointment dates or employment verification.
- Excel contains Companies, Product matches, Contact people, Contact routes and Export notes. Company filters determine the exported firms; all sourced people for those firms are retained, including additional roles. No outreach is sent by research or export.

## Evidence quality and gaps

No guessed emails, personal contact lookups, masked addresses or inferred current employment were imported. Hiring posts and liked/reposted posts do not establish that the profile owner holds the advertised role. Conflicting employers and historical procurement titles without adequate corroboration were excluded. Two Wingreens titles are retained as explicitly unverified directory leads, with separate LinkedIn company-association sources.

Partial product descriptions are labelled separately from full ingredient lists. The research corrected a false white-sugar match in “without processed white sugar” and retained only the supported palm jaggery. Explicit percentages preceding the ingredient were manually checked; cocoa percentages and nut percentages never become sugar percentages. All added products require current-label review. A shipping-weight audit corrected five new pack weights: three now use explicit product/variant titles and two remain unknown. Research correction timestamps are separate from original source check dates.

The [original catalogue crawl report](ingredient-refresh-report.json) records the earlier 97-source investigation (67 feeds, 6,800 listings, 2,439 cached product pages). The [supplemental catalogue pass](ingredient-catalogue-supplement-2026-09-30.json) and [current enrichment report](buyer-enrichment-report.json) document this expansion. Failed/inaccessible pages contribute no invented facts.

## Repeat collection and exports

`npm run research:buyers -- --company=mapro,cookie-man --pages` appends public catalogue matches. `--offline` uses the ignored local cache. This CLI keeps existing reviewed records, limits pagination and page attempts, and records failed sources. In-app scheduled automation remains limited to its seven established catalogue connectors; this manual research pass does not create a continuous LinkedIn or marketplace scraper.

`npm run research:export` regenerates the public Excel and CSV artifacts using only versioned research. It reads no .env or private CRM workspaces. The in-app Excel download includes the workspace data available to the current user.
